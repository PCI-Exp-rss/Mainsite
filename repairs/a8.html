Things to note for future repairs

> Phone is easily repairable

| All screws are identical

| Components such as dual front cameras and speaker and nfc coil are combined making dissasembly easier

| This does also make the phone less ecological and affordable to repair as in-case of failure of thoose components, it is required to replace the whole module/array of components.


Issues with this model's repairability

| This phone's IP-68 Ingress Protection / Water & Dust Resistance rating helps avoid repairs in the first place but makes them mre complicated when they are required due to the water-tight adhesive used on the screen and back cover.

| The charging port which is a component that will eventually fail due to normal wear and tear is soldered to the mainboard and requires advanced microsoldering to remove and replace once it does fail
